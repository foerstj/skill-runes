# skill-runes
Runes for Skills.

Provides runes which set your character stats to various levels. They override str/dex/int, the four skill levels, and the uber level.

Requires LoA. Based on the same mechanism that the skill scrolls in Return to Arhok are using.

WARNING: These runes override your character stats directly when you pick them up. Do NOT use them with your beloved legit level 100 character!

The runes are accessible via the provided map. They don't occur in pcontent.

Once your character stats are changed, progression continues as normal from there. If you bump your character up (or down) to level 20, you'll then need the same amount of XP to reach level 21 as if you had reached level 20 naturally, and also your str/dex/int will increase the same way.
